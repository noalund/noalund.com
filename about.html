noa lund

composer, artist

bio

My work has been described as “eclectic.” I’ve written chamber, orchestral, and electroacoustic scores; pieces involving staircases, tree branches, and computer algorithms; and, at times, found myself drawn into moments of pure conceptual or visual obsession, where sounds give way to videography, words, or other forms of media.

For years, I struggled to compress these varied interests into a coherent artistic identity—a task at which I’ve consistently failed. The publication of my work is, in part, an acceptance of this failure—a way of embracing my creations as they are—disparate, contradictory, and, above all, exploratory.

Beyond music and art, my interests include philosophy and technology, which have become increasingly significant influences on my work, helping me discover unconventional approaches to form.
